soft rotation is a concert series for experimental music and performance, organized by a small group of friends. Each edition, we try to bring into dialogue different perspectives on sound: our curation focuses on reoccurring accents, contrasting textures or deliberate counter-points. Apart from being involved in curation and organization, I am responsible for the current [visual identity](panel:soft_rotation_canv). Over the time, we've been lucky enough to host some amazing performances! If you're curious to find out more about the past editions and if you want to stay informed about upcoming events, follow us [here](https://substack.com/@softrotation?utm_campaign=profile&utm_medium=profile-page)! [Here](https://noies.nrw/gespraech-mit-soft-rotations-we-do-this-not-because-its-easy-but-because-we-thought-it-was-easy/) you'll find an interview we did with noies nrw.
