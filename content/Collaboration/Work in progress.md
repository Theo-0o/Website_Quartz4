Work in progress is a docu-fictional work by Alexandros Murböck and Karl Götter. The final sequence of the film describes a speculative scenario wherein the human is absent in nature. For this sequence I developed an auditive framework to run as a self-sustained, autonomous sound installation next to the work. Below is an excerpt of the work, the full sequence can be found [here](**https://youtu.be/alhzeg_AXFs**).
