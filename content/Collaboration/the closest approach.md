![[yotmwyfdftca_audio.mp4]]The installation depicts an artificially sweating bodily system that pumps fluid from the inside out into an industrial plant drip tray in a self-sustaining cycle. In parallel, a two-channel video depicts ant-like and humanoid body fragments in shifting, liminal states. Sculptures that appear to have emerged from the fictive close-up nature footage on display connect the digital and physical spaces. The work was developed in collaboration with Anna Janke and Milan Gress.
