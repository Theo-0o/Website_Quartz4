For some time now I've been interested in cones, conical horns and funnels, both for their shape and for their potential as an *instrument of listening*. I am particularly drawn to the idea that the cone simultaneously focuses and expands the personal perception of sound.

This led me to explore further how memory and perception shape sonic experience, even  before the actual occurrence of any sound. I tried to trace the processes that predate yet constitute this experience: attention, intuition, individual dispositions. To think about the temporality and the indivisibility of those aspects, the work leans into the notion of the not-yet.
